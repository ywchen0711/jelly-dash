# 果凍豆衝衝衝 Jelly Dash

An online party obstacle-course game for the browser. Up to 20 jelly beans race, dodge spinning bars and swinging hammers, smash through fake doors and survive falling tiles. Each round knocks some players out until one champion is left.

- **Solo:** play against computer beans.
- **Online:** one player creates a room and shares the 5-letter code or link; friends join from their own browsers. Empty spots are filled with computer players. Networking is peer-to-peer (WebRTC via PeerJS); the host's browser runs the show.
- **Rounds:** 旋轉大道 (race), 真假門門 (race), 跳跳轉盤 (survival) and the final 蜂巢墜落 (tile drop).
- **Controls:** WASD/arrows to move, Space to jump, Shift to dive, drag the mouse to turn the camera. On phones and tablets: drag on the left to move, drag on the right to look, buttons to jump and dive.

Built with three.js. No build step: serve the folder and open `index.html`.
